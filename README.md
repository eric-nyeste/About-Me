Philosophy | Human–AI Interaction | AI Evaluation Research

I'm a graduate researcher in Philosophy at San Francisco State University, investigating human agency, conversational control, and the evaluation of AI-mediated discourse.

My work explores how philosophical questions about epistemic agency, technological influence, and human judgment can be translated into empirical research methods.
