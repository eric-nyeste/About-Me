Beyond the Research

My philosophical interests extend through Nietzsche, Heidegger, Foucault, Lacan, and critical approaches to subjectivity, power, language, and technological mediation. I'm particularly interested in how philosophical concepts can inform empirical investigations without losing their conceptual complexity.

Outside academia, I have a longstanding involvement with motorcycles, both professionally and personally, particularly European motorcycles, mechanical engineering, design, and restoration.

I also write about food, culture, and the institutions and communities that give San Francisco its character.

These interests inform a broader curiosity about how people develop knowledge, exercise judgment, and establish meaningful relationships with the technologies and environments they inhabit.

Things to read:
Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert Pirsig : introduction to a philosophy of Quality
The Log from the Sea of Cortez - John Steinbeck : Chronicle of scientific exploration into non-teleological methodology.
The Glass Bead Game - Hermann Hesse : Where all the arts and sciences meet—and life begins.
The Formation of the Scientific Mind - Gaston Bachelard : Pedagogical framework for recognizing epistemic obstacles and the importance of rupture.
